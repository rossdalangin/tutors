# Sales & Marketing Strategy: EdupreneurPro

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Individual tutors and coaches.
- **Secondary**: Small to mid-sized training academies.
- **Tertiary**: Content creators moving into education.

## Channel Strategy
- **Content Marketing**: Weekly blog posts on "Scaling an Education Business" and "LMS Best Practices".
- **Social Media**: Short video tips on LinkedIn and Instagram.
- **Affiliate Outreach**: Partnering with WordPress influencers to review the plugin.
- **Email Marketing**: 5-day educational email sequence on "The Hidden Costs of Plugin Bloat".

## Sales Funnel
1. **Awareness**: Social media ads and SEO blog posts.
2. **Interest**: Lead magnet (Free Checklist: "Everything You Need to Start a 6-Figure Tutoring Business").
3. **Decision**: Product demo video and case studies.
4. **Action**: Yearly subscription offer with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
